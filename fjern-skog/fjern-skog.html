I Chapter one

The House

Fjern Skog is an old residence hidden deep within the forest, reached by a single track that the woods have half forgotten. It was built for those who wished to be elsewhere — and it has kept that promise for well over a century.

Inside, the rooms are low-lit and unhurried. Candles stand on worn oak, a fireplace holds the evening, and painted faces of long-departed owners regard the guest from heavy frames. It is designed for those seeking silence, nature and distance from everyday life.

No signal is promised. Only the sound of wind in the crowns, and a kettle.

II Chapter two

The Orchard

Behind the house the garden gave up long ago — and is better for it. Old apple, pear and plum trees lean into one another, their trunks furred with moss, the grass grown to the knee.

In autumn the fruit falls where it will. Walk slowly. Take what you like, and leave the rest to the deer.

III Chapter three

The Bath

A vintage freestanding bath sits beside a great window, and beyond the glass there is only forest. Fill it slowly. Let the light go blue, then grey, then out.

IV Chapter four

The Sauna

Down a short path, among the pines, stands a small wooden sauna — dark with smoke, intimate, made for two or three at most. Heat, birch and the smell of resin.

Afterwards, stand outside in the cold until the steam lifts off your shoulders and the forest returns.

V Chapter five

The Fireplace

Evenings gather around the hearth: a fire that must be tended, candles in pewter, a worn armchair with a book left open on its arm.

There is nowhere else to be.

VI Chapter six

The Details

Books, ceramics, paintings, candles, wood — small things that have outlived their owners.
